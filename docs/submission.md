## Review preparation

Validate the marketplace and plugin with `claude plugin validate --strict`. Review the [acceptance checks](acceptance.md) and record actual outcomes separately. Do not claim authenticated workflows have passed until they have been exercised in the target host.

Submit using the [Claude Console plugin form](https://platform.claude.com/plugins/submit). The [claude.ai organization form](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/directory/submissions/plugins/new) is another option for eligible organization administrators. Sign-in, organization access, and acceptance of directory terms must be completed by the publisher where required.

Public repository publication, direct marketplace installation, form submission, and directory approval are distinct states. Do not describe the plugin as approved or officially listed without confirmation from Anthropic.
